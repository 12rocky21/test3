📜 Notes for Every Mood

😊 When You’re Happy

  • “Every good and perfect gift is from above.” — James 1:17
  • “The Lord has done great things for us.” — Psalm 126:3
  • “Rejoice in the Lord always.” — Philippians 4:4

😌 When You Need Peace

  • “My peace I give you.” — John 14:27
  • “Be still, and know that I am God.” — Psalm 46:10
  • “The Lord blesses His people with peace.” — Psalm 29:11

😞 When You’re Sad

  • “The Lord is close to the brokenhearted.” — Psalm 34:18
  • “He heals the brokenhearted.” — Psalm 147:3
  • “Joy comes in the morning.” — Psalm 30:5

💪 When You Feel Weak

  • “I can do all things through Christ.” — Philippians 4:13
  • “His grace is sufficient.” — 2 Corinthians 12:9
  • “The Lord is my strength.” — Psalm 28:7

🌟 When You Need Courage

  • “Do not fear, for I am with you.” — Isaiah 41:10
  • “Be strong and courageous.” — Joshua 1:9
  • “The Lord is my helper; I will not be afraid.” — Hebrews 13:6

🧭 When You Need Guidance

  • “He will direct your paths.” — Proverbs 3:6
  • “Your word is a lamp to my feet.” — Psalm 119:105
  • “I will instruct you and teach you.” — Psalm 32:8

🌈 When You Need Hope

  • “For I know the plans I have for you.” — Jeremiah 29:11
  • “Hope in the Lord… He will renew your strength.” — Isaiah 40:31
  • “We have this hope as an anchor for the soul.” — Hebrews 6:19

🤍 When You Feel Alone

  • “I will never leave you nor forsake you.” — Hebrews 13:5
  • “I am with you always.” — Matthew 28:20
  • “You are precious in my sight.” — Isaiah 43:4

❤️ When You Need Comfort

  • “As a mother comforts her child, so will I comfort you.” — Isaiah 66:13
  • “He will wipe every tear.” — Revelation 21:4
  • “Cast all your cares on Him.” — 1 Peter 5:7

✨ When You’re Grateful

  • “Give thanks to the Lord, for He is good.” — Psalm 107:1
  • “In everything give thanks.” — 1 Thessalonians 5:18
  • “Bless the Lord, O my soul.” — Psalm 103:1

🌱 When You Need Patience

  • “Wait for the Lord; be strong.” — Psalm 27:14
  • “Those who wait on the Lord shall renew their strength.” — Isaiah 40:31
  • “Love is patient.” — 1 Corinthians 13:4

🔥 When You Feel Lost

  • “The Lord is my shepherd.” — Psalm 23:1
  • “He restores my soul.” — Psalm 23:3
  • “Call to me and I will answer you.” — Jeremiah 33:3

🌤 When You’re Stressed

  • “Come to me… I will give you rest.” — Matthew 11:28
  • “Cast your burden on the Lord.” — Psalm 55:22
  • “My grace is sufficient.” — 2 Corinthians 12:9

🤲 When You Need Forgiveness

  • “If we confess our sins, He forgives.” — 1 John 1:9
  • “As far as the east is from the west…” — Psalm 103:12
  • “There is no condemnation for those in Christ.” — Romans 8:1

✝️ When You Need Strength to Keep Going

  • “The Lord will fight for you.” — Exodus 14:14
  • “Do not grow weary in doing good.” — Galatians 6:9
  • “My power is made perfect in weakness.” — 2 Corinthians 12:9

😟 When You’re Anxious

  • “Cast all your anxiety on Him.” — 1 Peter 5:7
  • “My peace I give you… do not be afraid.” — John 14:27
  • “Your consolation brought me joy.” — Psalm 94:19

😠 When You’re Angry

  • “Be slow to anger.” — James 1:19
  • “A gentle answer turns away wrath.” — Proverbs 15:1
  • “Do not let the sun go down while you are still angry.” — Ephesians 4:26

😌 When You’re Feeling Proud (healthy pride)

  • “Let the one who boasts boast in the Lord.” — Jeremiah 9:24
  • “Every good and perfect gift is from above.” — James 1:17
  • “Humble yourselves… and He will lift you up.” — James 4:10

😎 When You’re Too Hyped / Emotionally High

  • “Be still before the Lord.” — Psalm 37:7
  • “In quietness and trust is your strength.” — Isaiah 30:15
  • “Set your mind on things above.” — Colossians 3:2

😎 When You’re Disappointed

  • “The Lord is good to those whose hope is in Him.” — Lamentations 3:25
  • “Blessed is the one who trusts in the Lord.” — Jeremiah 17:7
  • “Cast your burden on the Lord.” — Psalm 55:22

🤸 When You Feel Like Not Exercising

  • “Do you not know that your bodies are temples of the Holy Spirit?” — 1 Corinthians 6:19
  • “For physical training is of some value, but godliness has value for all things.” — 1 Timothy 4:8
  • “She sets about her work vigorously; her arms are strong for her tasks.” — Proverbs 31:17
  • “Dear friend, I pray that you may enjoy good health and that all may go well with you.” — 3 John 1:2
  • “Do not be wise in your own eyes; fear the Lord and shun evil. This will bring health to your body and nourishment to your bones.” — Proverbs 3:7-8
